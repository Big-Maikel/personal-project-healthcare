# Data Dictionary for Patient Appointment Dataset

## patients.csv
- Description: One row per patient
- Columns:
  - patient_id (str): Unique identifier for the patient
  - age (int): Age of the patient
  - preferred_language (category): Patient's preferred language for communication
  - distance_km (float): Approximate distance from patient's home to clinic
  - has_transport (category): Whether the patient has access to transport
  - chronic_conditions_count (int): Number of chronic conditions
  - patient_since_year (int): Year patient's record was first created

## appointments.csv
- Description: One row per appointment
- Columns:
  - appointment_id (str): Appointment identifier
  - patient_id (str): Patient identifier (join key)
  - department (category): Clinic department
  - appointment_type (category): Type/purpose of appointment
  - scheduled_date (date): Date appointment is scheduled for
  - booking_lead_days (int): Days between booking and appointment
  - weekday (category): Day of week for appointment
  - appointment_status (category): Final operational status
  - duration_minutes (int): Length in minutes (regression target)

## reminders.csv
- Description: Reminder information (joined by appointment_id)
- Columns:
  - appointment_id (str): Join key to appointments
  - sms_sent (category): Whether SMS reminder was sent
  - days_before_reminder (int/category): Days before appointment reminder was sent
  - reminder_language (category): Language used in reminder message

## appointment_outcomes.csv
- Description: Target labels (joined by appointment_id)
- Columns:
  - appointment_id (str): Join key to appointments
  - missed_appointment (0/1): Binary indicator of missed appointment
